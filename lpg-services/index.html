FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LPG?

LPG (liquefied petroleum gas) is a bottled gas used where properties are not connected to mains natural gas. It is commonly used on static caravans, holiday homes, park homes and rural residential properties for heating, hot water and cooking.

Do I need an annual LPG service?

Yes — annual servicing is strongly recommended to keep appliances safe and efficient, maintain warranties where applicable, and identify wear before it becomes a fault. Holiday parks and landlords often require evidence of regular checks.

Can you work on static caravans?

Yes. We provide specialist LPG services for static caravans and lodges, including servicing, safety inspections, repairs and new appliance installations on holiday parks across Morecambe, Lancaster and surrounding areas.

Do you provide LPG safety certificates?

We carry out LPG safety checks and can issue landlord gas safety records (CP12) where required for rental properties and holiday lets with LPG installations.

Are your engineers Gas Safe registered for LPG work?

Our engineers are Gas Safe registered with the appropriate qualifications for LPG work. Always check the engineer's ID card and the work categories listed before any gas work begins.
